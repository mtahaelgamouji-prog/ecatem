About

ECATEM is a Moroccan company with two core activities: tent manufacturing in its own workshop, and theimport of shade products (parasols, pergolas, awnings and shade sails).

Our team of seamstresses, welders and assembly technicians designs shelters suited to every use. Depending on the product and the order value, we can also handle installation and assembly.

1976 - 2026

50 years of tradition, innovation and creativity. Made in Morocco: the art of a job well done

For 50 years, El Haj Ibrahim Aouad has put his expertise into building shelters. In 1993, he founded ECATEM: our tents are designed, cut and sewn in our Témara workshop, with materials chosen to last in the Moroccan climate.

Our story

More than thirty years of tent manufacturing in Morocco, and a company that has always been there when the country needed shelters.

  1. 1993

    Foundation

    ECATEM is founded

    Mr. Ibrahim Aouad founds ECATEM and starts manufacturing tents in Morocco.

  2. [year]

    Growth

    Settling in Témara

    ECATEM moves to the Témara industrial zone, home to its workshop and head office.

  3. [year]

    Diversification

    Import activity begins

    The company adds imported parasols, pergolas, awnings and shade sails to its range.

  4. [year]

    Partnerships

    Renowned suppliers

    ECATEM works with leading manufacturers of fabrics, structures and motors.

  5. 2020

    Covid-19 pandemic

    At work throughout the pandemic

    During the Covid-19 pandemic, our teams kept working to make and prepare tents for health and logistics needs, while following protective measures.

  6. 2023

    Al Haouz earthquake · September 2023

    Working day and night for those affected

    In the aftermath of the 8 September 2023 earthquake, our teams worked day and night in our Témara workshop to make and prepare, as quickly as possible, tents for the families affected in the Al Haouz region.

  7. Today

    33 years of experience

    Manufacturer and importer

    ECATEM makes custom tents and offers a wide range of parasols, pergolas and awnings, with 16 parasol models in stock.
