Care tips

Maintenance

Make your fabrics and frames last longer

A few simple steps keep your parasols, awnings, tents and pergolas in good condition, season after season.

Parasol, awning and sail fabricsWind and bad weatherTents and PVC fabricsAluminium and steel framesPergolasOff-season storage
01

Parasol, awning and sail fabrics

  • Regularly brush the dry fabric with a soft brush.
  • For a thorough clean, use lukewarm water, mild soap and a sponge, then rinse well with clean water.
  • Do not use pressure washers, bleach, solvents or hard brushes: they damage the fabric and its coating.
  • Always let the fabric dry completely, open, before closing or storing it.
  • Remove stains (bird droppings, resin, leaves) quickly so they do not set in.
02

Wind and bad weather

  • Close parasols and retract awnings as soon as the wind picks up: it is the main cause of breakage.
  • Do not leave a parasol open unattended, at night or while you are away.
  • Check that the base is heavy enough for the size of the parasol.
  • After rain, unroll the awning as soon as possible to let it dry.
  • For shade sails, keep enough slope for water to run off.
03

Tents and PVC fabrics

  • Clean PVC fabric with soapy water and a soft sponge, then rinse.
  • Never fold a damp tent: dry it completely to avoid mould and odours.
  • Fold the fabric without always creasing it in the same places and away from sharp metal parts.
  • Store it in a dry, ventilated place, away from sun and rodents.
  • Each time you put it up, check the straps, guy ropes, pegs and fabric tension.
04

Aluminium and steel frames

  • Clean poles and frames with soapy water, without abrasive products.
  • Regularly check that screws, bolts and joints are tight.
  • Lightly lubricate the mechanisms (crank, pulleys, folding arms) with a suitable product if needed.
  • By the sea, rinse more often with clean water to remove salt.
05

Pergolas

  • Regularly clear leaves and debris from the louvres and gutters.
  • Check that water drains properly through the downpipes.
  • On a motorised pergola, never force the louvres and have the motor checked if it makes an unusual noise.
06

Off-season storage

  • Clean and dry parasols, fabrics and sails before storing them.
  • Use a breathable protective cover.
  • Store equipment under cover, in a dry, ventilated room.

These are general tips. Also follow the manufacturer's instructions supplied with your product.
