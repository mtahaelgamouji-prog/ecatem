About ECATEM

Sheltering your projects for more than thirty years

Founded in 1993 in Témara, ECATEM specialises in tents, awnings, parasols and pergolas for businesses, public bodies and homeowners. Drawing on 50 years of expertise passed on by its founder, El Haj Ibrahim Aouad, it offers custom, durable solutions suited to the Moroccan climate, from project design to delivery.

Our commitments

Why choose ECATEM?

Since 1993, we have supported businesses, public bodies and private clients with the same high standards: sturdy shelters, suited to the Moroccan climate and supported over time.

  1. 01

    Made to measure

    Every tent is made in our workshop to the dimensions and colours of your project.

  2. 02

    Sun protection

    Our fabrics and shade products are UV-treated and designed for the Moroccan climate.

  3. 03

    Wide range

    Tents, hangars, parasols, pergolas, awnings and sails: one address for all your shelters.

  4. 04

    Advice and after-sales service

    We help you choose the right product. Installation and assembly are offered for some products, depending on the order value.
