We are ECATEM

ECATEM designs and makes tents, and supplies awnings, parasols and pergolas that meet high standards of quality and durability.

Founded in 1993 in Témara, the company draws on 50 years of know-how handed down by its founder, El Haj Ibrahim Aouad. Experience, rigour and a sense of service are the foundations of our success.

As a family business, we work with a strong team and leading manufacturers of fabrics and structures. In our factories in Témara and Tamesna, we design your made-to-measure shelter, true to our motto: the art of a job well done.
