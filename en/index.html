About ECATEM

Sheltering your projects for more than thirty years

Founded in 1993 in Témara, ECATEM specialises in tents, awnings, parasols and pergolas for businesses, public bodies and homeowners. Drawing on 50 years of expertise passed on by its founder, El Haj Ibrahim Aouad, it offers custom, durable solutions suited to the Moroccan climate, from project design to delivery.
