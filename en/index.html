About us:

Our motto “Your shelter, our expertise” sums up how we work: listen to every client, make to measure and install with care, from a small stand to a large hangar.

Learn more

The founder

Mr. Ibrahim Aouad

Founder and Managing Director of ECATEM

I founded ECATEM on a simple belief: a shelter must be strong, well finished and delivered on time. Every tent that leaves our workshop carries that standard, and every client deserves the same care, whatever the size of their project.

Under his leadership, ECATEM has been manufacturing custom tents and importing shade solutions for businesses and individuals across Morocco since 1993.
